Guide mode · from Lecture 7 slides 22 to 36 and the rubric

Q3 is asking you to separate what the garage does from how well it does it.

FunctionalSomething the system must do. "The system shall display the number of free spaces at each entrance."
Non-functionalA quality it must have while doing it. "The free-space count shall update within 2 seconds of a car entering."

Quick test: if you can picture a driver pressing a button or seeing a result, it is functional. If it is about speed, safety, reliability or cost, it is non-functional.

The rubric also wants two things per requirement: which stakeholder need it traces to (your Q1 list), and how you would verify it, by test, inspection or demonstration.

Your turn: write one of each for the garage and I will check them against the slide 24 checklist.

第 3 题要你区分系统做什么和做得多好。

Functional · 功能性需求系统必须完成的事。"系统应在每个入口显示空余车位数。"
Non-functional · 非功能性需求完成任务时必须具备的品质。"车辆进入后，空位数应在 2 秒内更新。"

快速判断：如果你能想象司机按下按钮或看到结果，就是功能性需求；如果说的是速度、安全、可靠性或成本，就是非功能性需求。

评分标准还要求每条需求写明两点：它追溯 (traceability) 到你第 1 题列出的哪个利益相关者需求，以及如何验证 (verify)，例如测试、检查或演示。

轮到你了：各写一条，我会按第 24 页的清单帮你检查。
