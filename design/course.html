What this lecture covers

2 points flagged for the quiz
1Elicitation means discovering what stakeholders need, not only what they say they want. The professor frames it as a conversation, not a questionnaire.
2Four techniques are compared, interviews, workshops, observation and document analysis, with when each works best (slides 8 to 19).
3The biggest risk is gold plating: adding features nobody asked for. Three slides on this, and in the recording at 42:10 he says "this will show up on the quiz". Quiz
4A good requirement is verifiable, unambiguous and traceable to a stakeholder need. The checklist on slide 24 is worth memorizing. Quiz
5The last section walks through the parking garage example, the same system HW 2 question 3 asks about. On Ed, the professor confirmed yesterday that non-functional requirements need a stakeholder too. Thread #142

Key terms · kept in English, translated once

Elicitation需求获取Drawing out needs from stakeholders through interviews, workshops or observation.
Traceability可追溯性Every requirement links back to a need and forward to a test.
Gold plating镀金（过度设计）Adding scope no stakeholder asked for; a cost and schedule risk.

Stakeholder利益相关者Anyone who affects or is affected by the system, not only the customer.
