Q3: do non-functional requirements need a stakeholder too?

Ed thread #142 · HW 2 · asked yesterday · professor answered 4 h ago · 9 replies
S Student (anonymous) · yesterday 9:14 PM

For Q3 the prompt says to "state the stakeholder need it traces to" for each requirement. Does that apply to non-functional requirements as well, or only to the functional ones? Non-functional ones like "update within 2 seconds" do not seem to belong to any one stakeholder.

P [Instructor name] Endorsed answer · 4 h ago

Yes, both. Every requirement, functional or not, must trace to a stakeholder need. For non-functional requirements the stakeholder is usually the operator or the owner: "update within 2 seconds" traces to the garage operator's need for accurate occupancy at the gates. If you cannot name the stakeholder, the requirement is probably gold plating. See slide 24.
